A coworker just told you some juicy gossip. You leaned in. Now they'll tell your secrets too.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Gossip is a loyalty test. Whoever brings it to you is already carrying yours to someone else.

Move one: listen, but don't add. Say "huh, really?" and change the subject. Nothing you said, nothing to repeat.

Move two: never put it in writing. One screenshot of your chat message travels further than anything you said at lunch.

Move three: answer gossip with a compliment. "Really? She saved me on the budget deck last month." Now the story that travels makes you look good.

Gossip makes you popular for a week. Discretion makes you trusted for years.
