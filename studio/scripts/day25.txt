Your project is going to be late. Your boss doesn't know yet. And every day you wait, it becomes more your fault.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Bosses forgive bad news. They never forgive surprises.

Move one: tell them the day you know, not the day it's due. "Heads up, the vendor slipped. Launch is at risk."

Move two: bring two options with the problem. "We cut the reporting page, or we move launch to the twentieth. Which do you prefer?"

Move three: put it in writing. After the chat, send a three line recap. What changed, what you agreed, the new date.

Bad news early is a heads up. Bad news late is a confession.
