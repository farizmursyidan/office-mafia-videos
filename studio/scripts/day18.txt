You just got a new boss. Your track record just reset to zero. And someone else is already in their office.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. A new boss doesn't read your history. They believe whatever they hear first.

Move one: get there first. In week one, book fifteen minutes. Say what you own and one win. "I run client renewals. We kept all twelve accounts."

Move two: ask what winning means. "What would make your first ninety days great?" Write down the answer. That's your real job description now.

Move three: hand them a quick win. Find the report they keep complaining about. Fix it by Friday, before anyone asks.

Your old boss knew your work. Your new boss knows your first week.
