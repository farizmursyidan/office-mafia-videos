You just started a new job. Everyone is being really nice. And they've already decided where you rank.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Your first thirty days become your reputation. Everything after that gets filed under it.

Move one: find out who people actually ask. Not the org chart. Notice who everyone messages when something breaks, and buy that person coffee in week one.

Move two: get one small win people can see. Fix the outdated onboarding doc, then share it with the whole team.

Move three: never say "at my old company." Ask why things work the way they do before you suggest changing anything.

First impressions fade. First labels stick.
