Your boss just gave you a special project. Nobody else wanted it. And that's exactly why it's yours.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Some projects are promotions. Others are parking spots. And they look the same on day one.

Move one: find the sponsor. Ask one question. "Which VP asked for this?" If nobody can name one, nobody is waiting for it.

Move two: check the budget. No money, no headcount, no launch date? That's not a priority. That's a parking spot.

Move three: set an end date. Tell your boss, "I'll run this for eight weeks, then we review it together." Now the project has an exit. And so do you.

Good projects find sponsors. Bad projects find volunteers.
