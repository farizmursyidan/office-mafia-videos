The most powerful person in your office isn't on the org chart. It's whoever controls your boss's calendar.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Every decision maker has a gatekeeper. Win them over, or you never get in.

Move one: learn their name and use it. The assistant, the chief of staff, the person who books the rooms. Treat them like a peer, not a receptionist.

Move two: make their job easy. Ask for fifteen minutes, give the agenda, offer two time slots. Gatekeepers open doors for people who make their day simple.

Move three: never go around them. Messaging the boss directly to skip the line works once. After that, every door quietly closes.

Titles decide who signs. Gatekeepers decide who gets the meeting.
