Your boss just messaged you at nine at night. You replied in two minutes. Now they expect that every night.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Your reply time is a promise. The speed you show once becomes the standard.

Move one: answer late messages in the morning. At eight thirty, write "On it. You'll have it by noon." Same answer, no new standard.

Move two: sort the urgent from the loud. If it's truly urgent, they'll call. A message at nine is usually a thought they didn't want to forget.

Move three: say your hours once, out loud. In your next one on one: "After seven I'm offline. Call me if something's on fire."

Instant replies make you available. Clear hours make you respected.
