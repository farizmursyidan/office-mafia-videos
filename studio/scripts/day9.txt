Every email you send at work has a second message hidden in the CC line. And everyone reads it.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. The CC line isn't for information. It's for witnesses.

Move one: only CC up when it protects you. Copying someone's boss on a small mistake doesn't fix it. It starts a war you will lose.

Move two: when someone CCs your manager to corner you, reply fast, calm, and with facts. Never reply all angry. Answer the question, attach the proof, done.

Move three: send praise up, keep problems down. Thank a teammate with their boss on CC. Raise a problem one on one, before it hits an inbox.

The To line asks for something. The CC line says who's watching.
