You're going on vacation next week. Someone is covering for you. And they might not give your work back.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Whoever holds your work while you're gone decides who gets the credit.

Move one: hand over tasks, not relationships. Give them the open tickets, but tell your biggest client, "I'm back on the twelfth. Sam has the small stuff."

Move two: write the handover doc yourself, and send it to your boss too. One page. What's done, what's in progress, and what's yours when you return.

Move three: book the catch up before you leave. Thirty minutes with your boss on your first morning back, so the update comes from you, not from Sam.

Covering is helping. Keeping is taking.
