Your self review is due Friday. You're going to be modest. And your boss is going to copy it word for word.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Your manager has twelve reviews to write this week. Whatever you hand them becomes your rating.

Move one: write evidence, not effort. Not "I worked hard on the launch." Write "I shipped the launch two weeks early and cut support tickets by a third."

Move two: use the words of the next level. If senior means owning a project end to end, write "I owned the billing project end to end."

Move three: end with what you want next. One line. "Next year I want to lead our biggest client account."

Modest gets forgotten. Specific gets promoted.
