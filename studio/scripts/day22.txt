Your boss's boss just booked fifteen minutes with you. Your boss wasn't invited. And every word will get back to them.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. A skip level isn't a chat. It's an audition. And your boss reads the review.

Move one: bring one win with a number. Not "things are going well." Say, "We cut invoice errors by thirty percent."

Move two: make your boss look good. "My manager pushed us to fix it." Credit that flows up comes back to you.

Move three: ask a question only they can answer. "What's the one problem keeping you up this quarter?" Then offer to help with it.

Complainers get remembered. Helpers get promoted.
