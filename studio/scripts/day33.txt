Someone just called out your mistake in the team chat. Forty people saw it. And they're all waiting for your reply.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Nobody remembers the mistake. They remember how you reacted to it.

Move one: reply fast, short and calm. "Good catch. Fixing it by three." No excuses, no long paragraph.

Move two: take the rest private. If they were rude, message them directly. "Next time, can you flag it to me first?" Never argue where your boss can scroll.

Move three: close the loop in public. When it's fixed, post "Done. Updated file attached." Now the last word in the thread is yours.

Defensive replies get screenshotted. Calm replies get respected.
