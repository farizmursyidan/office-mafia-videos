Your boss just got asked about your project. They had nothing to say. And that's on you.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Your boss's boss only sees you through your boss. If your boss can't explain your work, it didn't happen.

Move one: send the update before they ask. Every Friday, three lines. Done, next, blocked.

Move two: bring options, not problems. The vendor slipped a week? Here's plan A, plan B, and I'd pick B.

Move three: learn how they want bad news. Some want a quick message, some want a two minute call. Nobody wants a surprise.

Doing the work gets you paid. Managing up gets you promoted.
