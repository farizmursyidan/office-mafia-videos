The real decision in your office was made after the meeting. In the hallway. And you weren't there.

Here's how it actually works. Meetings are where decisions get announced. They get made before and after.

Move one: pre-wire. Before any meeting where you need a yes, talk to the decision maker alone first. Nobody likes being surprised in front of other people.

Move two: stay for the meeting after the meeting. Walk out with the most senior person. Grab a coffee. Ask one question: so what did you really think?

Move three: write the recap. Whoever writes the summary decides what was agreed. Send it within the hour.

The meeting is the show. The hallway is where the deal gets done.
