You're mid-sentence in a meeting. Someone cuts you off. And the whole room lets them.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Every interruption is a test. Let one slide, and the room learns your ideas can wait.

Move one: keep talking. Don't stop, don't speed up. Just say their name. "Mark, one second, let me finish."

Move two: line up an ally. Before the meeting, ask a friend to step in. "Wait, I want to hear the rest of that." It lands harder from someone else.

Move three: take the floor back. When they finally stop, say "Going back to my point," and finish like nothing happened.

Polite people get talked over. Calm people get the last word.
