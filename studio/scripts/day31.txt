Your office party is next month. You're planning to skip it. And your boss's boss will notice who showed up.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. The party isn't a break from work. It's a meeting with no agenda, where leaders size people up.

Move one: arrive early, leave early. The first hour is when the bosses are there. When the music gets loud, they leave. So do you.

Move two: talk to one leader you never see. Ask one easy question, like "What are you most excited about next year?" Then listen.

Move three: hold one drink all night. Nobody gets promoted for a great story from the party. Plenty get passed over for one.

The work gets you noticed. The party gets you remembered.
