You've been waiting to get promoted. Your boss thinks you're happy where you are. And the coworker who asked got your spot.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Bosses don't hand out promotions. They approve the ones already on the table. If you never ask, you're not on it.

Move one: say the title out loud. Book thirty minutes and name it. "I want senior analyst by June."

Move two: ask what's missing. "What would you need to see to put me up for it?" Send the answer back in writing. Now it's on record.

Move three: write their pitch. One page, three wins, real numbers. Your boss has to sell you in a room you're not in.

Waiting makes you look patient. Asking makes you look ready.
