A reorg is never a surprise. The people who survive it saw the signs three months earlier.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Reorgs don't reward whoever works hardest. They reward whoever the new boss already knows.

Sign one: meetings vanish without a reason. Your weekly sync with leadership disappears from the calendar.

Sign two: new names show up. A consultant you've never met is suddenly CC'd on every budget email.

Sign three: your boss goes vague. Ask about next quarter and you hear, let's see how things shake out.

Spot two, and move. Write down your three biggest wins, and get fifteen minutes with your boss's boss.

Loyalty to the old chart gets you moved. Visibility on the new one gets you kept.
