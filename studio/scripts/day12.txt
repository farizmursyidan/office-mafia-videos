Every time you say yes to extra work, you tell your boss your real job wasn't full.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. The people who get promoted don't say yes more. They say no better.

Move one: don't say no, say which. Sure, I can take the vendor report. Which of my three projects should slip?

Move two: put a price on it. Yes, I can build the deck. It means Friday's launch moves to Monday. Let them decide if it's worth it.

Move three: say no to the task, yes to the person. I can't own this, but I'll spend twenty minutes showing Sam how I'd start.

Saying yes to everything makes you helpful. Saying no well makes you senior.
