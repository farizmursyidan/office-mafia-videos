You worked from home all week. You got more done than anyone. And your boss still thinks you were coasting.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Managers don't reward the work. They reward the work they saw happen.

Move one: come in when the deciders do. Check your boss's calendar. If they're in the office on Tuesday, so are you.

Move two: make your wins visible. When something ships, post two lines in the team channel. "Client deck sent. They signed off."

Move three: show up early on video calls. Join two minutes before the start and talk to whoever is there. That's your hallway now.

Hard work happens at your desk. Promotions happen in the hallway.
