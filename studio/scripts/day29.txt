Your boss just asked for honest feedback. You're about to give it. And they'll remember it longer than your work.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. When a boss asks for honesty, they're not asking for a list. They're checking whose side you're on.

Move one: give one small thing they can fix this week. "The Monday meeting could end ten minutes early." Not a review of their whole style.

Move two: tie it to their win. "If we sent the agenda on Friday, your update to the VP would land sharper."

Move three: treat the anonymous survey like it has your name on it. On a team of five, they'll guess who wrote it.

Careful feedback makes you an ally. Careless feedback makes you a risk.
