You just quit your job. HR booked your exit interview. And they swear it's confidential.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. An exit interview doesn't fix the company. It writes your reference.

Move one: praise people, criticize processes. Say "Maya taught me so much, and promotions here need clearer criteria." Not "My boss is the reason I'm leaving."

Move two: assume every word gets forwarded. If you wouldn't say it to your boss's face, don't put it on the form.

Move three: say goodbye in person before your last day. Ten minutes with your boss, and ten with your boss's boss. Thank each of them for one specific thing.

You're leaving the job. Your reputation is staying.
