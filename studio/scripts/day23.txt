The new hire on your team makes more than you. You trained them. And nobody is going to fix it for you.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Raises aren't decided at your review. They're decided months earlier, when the budget gets set.

Move one: know your number. Pull three job posts for your exact role and city. Bring the middle one.

Move two: ask before the budget closes. Not at your review in March. In the fall, while there's still money on the table.

Move three: make it their problem to solve. Say, "I want to stay here. This gap makes that hard. What can you do by January?"

Loyalty gets you thanked. Leverage gets you paid.
