You just got asked to take the notes. Again. And that's why you're not getting promoted.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Some work keeps the team running. Other work gets you promoted. They're almost never the same.

Move one: rotate the chore. Don't refuse. Say, "Happy to do it today. Who's got next week?" Now it's a rotation, not your job.

Move two: trade up. Give one chore away, ask for one visible job. "I'll hand off the onboarding docs. Let me present the client update."

Move three: put a number on what's left. One line in your weekly update. "Trained three new hires, cut setup to two days."

Helpful keeps you busy. Visible gets you promoted.
