Someone on your team wants your promotion. They started campaigning last month. And you haven't noticed yet.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. A promotion isn't a race. It's a comparison. And your rival is already shaping the comparison.

Move one: never fight them in public. When they take a shot at your plan in a meeting, say "Good point. Let's dig in after." Staying calm looks senior.

Move two: own the work they avoid. They grab the shiny launch? You take the angry client nobody can keep happy, and you fix it.

Move three: make your boss's choice easy. Every Friday, send one line. "This week I closed the Acme renewal."

Rivals win with noise. You win with receipts.
