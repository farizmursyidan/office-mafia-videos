Your performance review was decided months ago. The meeting is just where they read it to you.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. Your rating gets argued in a calibration room you're not in. Your manager needs ammunition to fight for you.

Move one: keep a brag doc. Every Friday, write one win with a number. Cut onboarding from two weeks to five days. Your manager forgets. The doc doesn't.

Move two: ask in month nine, not month twelve. What would make this a top rating? Then do exactly that, and tell them.

Move three: send your manager a summary before calibration. Three wins, three numbers, one page. Make it easy to copy and paste.

Hard work earns the rating. A written record defends it.
