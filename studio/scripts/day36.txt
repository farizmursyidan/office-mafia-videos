Your teammate did nothing on the group project. You covered for them. And now you're both getting the same rating.

Here's the rule nobody tells you. When you fix someone's work in silence, their problem quietly becomes your job.

Move one: put names on the work from day one. In the kickoff doc, write "Deck: Dan. Data: you." Now everyone can see who owns what.

Move two: raise it as a deadline risk, not a complaint. Say "The client section is still empty and we present Thursday. Can Dan confirm by tomorrow?"

Move three: if you step in, say it out loud. Post "I finished the client section so we hit Thursday."

Covering quietly makes you a doormat. Covering out loud makes you a leader.
